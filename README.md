# BestyKitchen

**The recipe and spice platform built for the Best Ever Food Review Show audience.**

Best Ever Food Review Show has over 10 million subscribers and a deeply engaged global audience that watches Sonny travel the world eating food they have never tried. Every episode ends the same way — viewers want to cook what they just watched, and they have nowhere to go, at least that's what happens to me.

BestyKitchen fills that gap. I am building the companion platform that turns BEFRS episodes into actionable recipes and turns those recipes into a spice commerce business if it gets that far.

---

## What am I Building

**1. Recipe Archive**
Every dish featured on Best Ever Food Review Show, converted into a home-cook-friendly recipe with full ingredients, step-by-step instructions, and a direct link back to the episode. Searchable and filterable by country and region.

**2. Spice Store** *(next phase if it passes)*
The single biggest barrier between a viewer and a recipe is sourcing the right spices. We will be building a curated spice store that ships international spice kits — Mexican, Ethiopian, Vietnamese, Indonesian — directly to customers across Africa and globally. Get the recipe, buy the spices, cook the dish.

**3. Email List**
Every visitor is offered the chance to receive new recipes by email. This list is our direct line to the audience and the foundation for every product launch.

---

## Why This Works

- The audience already exists. BEFRS has 10M+ subscribers who are pre-sold on the food, me included.
- The demand already exists. Viewers search for these recipes after every episode with nowhere official to land.
- The product sells itself. A spice kit tied to a specific episode and dish is a story, not just a product.
- The African market is underserved. Getting Mexican or Vietnamese spices in Nairobi is nearly impossible. We should solve that.


## Current Stage

This is a working prototype built to demonstrate the concept to the BEFRS team. It is functional, designed in BEFRS brand colours, and ready to show.

The next conversation is a partnership — content rights, co-branding, and a revenue share model on spice sales.

## Stack

Pure HTML, CSS, JavaScript — no framework, no backend, no database. Intentionally lean. Deployable anywhere in seconds.

Hosted on GitHub Pages at zero cost until the business warrants infrastructure investment.
